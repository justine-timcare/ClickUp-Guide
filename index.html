ClickUp — getting started

For anyone opening ClickUp for the first time. Where things are, how to read the dashboard, and how to keep the monthly numbers right.

OneThe screen

This is the SPM Segmentation Master Dashboard. Six things to find before anything else.

1 · Sidebar
The icon strip down the left. Dashboard is the one you'll use — click the » at the top to see the labels.
2 · Open tabs
Each dashboard you open gets a tab: the Master, then one per SPM. Switch between them here.
3 · Share & export
Schedule report, Share, and the download arrow. Full-screen is the arrow beside them.
4 · Edit mode
Off by default, and it should stay off. On, you can move or delete cards for everyone.
5 · Refresh & filters
When the data last updated, whether auto refresh is on, and the filters over the whole page.
6 · A card
One chart. Its small grey subtitle tells you which field it's counting — this matters more than the title.
Leave Edit mode alone

Nothing on a dashboard is private. Turning on Edit mode and dragging a card moves it for everyone who opens it. You never need it just to read the numbers.

TwoWhere the numbers actually live

A card doesn't hold any data. It counts up what's stored on tasks, and redraws itself. So the chart is only ever as good as the tasks underneath.

So the 1,005 on Bryan's June card is every June figure across every corporation in Bryan's List, added together.

A check you can do in ten seconds

Q2 Total is typed in by hand, not calculated. So the Q2 card should always equal the three month cards added up. Read them across for one SPM:

FourIs it current?

Top right of the dashboard, next to the filters.

  1. Refreshed 11 mins ago tells you how old the figures are. Click it to refresh now.
  2. Auto refresh: On means it updates itself. If it's off, refresh manually before reading anything.
  3. Filters applies across every card at once. Check it before deciding a total looks wrong.

Unlike a spreadsheet, you never save a dashboard. Change a task and the cards catch up on their own.

FiveUpdating the monthly numbers

The recurring job. It happens in the List, not on the dashboard — the dashboard just reflects it afterwards.

  1. In the sidebar, open Tim Care Projects › SPM Segmentation Q2 2026 › the SPM's List.
  2. Make sure you're on the List tab at the top, not Board or Calendar. Only List view lets you type into the number columns.
  3. If the month column isn't showing, click the + at the far right of the column headers and switch it on.
  4. Click the cell where the corporation's row meets the month, and type the number. Digits only — 1054, not 1,054.
  5. 5Press Enter to save and drop to the next row. There's no save button; ClickUp saves as you go.
  6. Add the three months and type the result into Q2 Total. It does not calculate itself.
Blank is not zero

Leave a month blank if a corporation wasn't active yet. A blank means "not started"; a 0 means "active but shipped nothing" — and that difference is what someone will ask about when volume dips.

SixIf something looks wrong

A card shows zeroIt's usually pointed at one List instead of the whole Folder, or a filter is on. Don't edit the card — tell whoever owns the dashboard.
Q2 Total doesn't match the monthsA task's Q2 Total wasn't updated. Fix it in the List and the card follows.
A number won't saveNumber fields reject commas and words. Type 1054, not "1,054 kits".
The month column isn't thereIt's hidden, not missing. Use the + at the end of the column headers.
A task vanishedCheck if it was archived rather than deleted — the List's ⋯ menu has "Show archived". Deleted tasks sit in Trash for 30 days.
You changed something by accidentOpen the task and scroll to the activity feed at the bottom. It shows the old value, who changed it, and when.
